About the recognition#

This Certificate of Achievement recognises sincere involvement as a Mentor in the “Machine Learning for Research Bootcamp 24”, conducted from October 2024 to April 2025. The bootcamp was organised by the Research & Journal Wing of the Computer & Programming Club (CPC) at Daffodil International University.

The certificate notes that the guidance, motivation and mentorship provided were instrumental in empowering participants and enhancing the overall learning experience of the bootcamp.

  • Organised by: Research & Journal Wing, Computer & Programming Club, Daffodil International University
  • Period: October 2024 to April 2025
  • Signed by: Prof. Dr. Sheak Rashed Haider Noori (Professor and Head, Department of CSE), Mayen Uddin Mojumdar (Convener, DIU CPC), Md. Mizanur Rahman (Advisor, R&J Wing, DIU CPC) and Deawan Rakin Ahamed Remal (Co-Advisor, R&J Wing, DIU CPC)
